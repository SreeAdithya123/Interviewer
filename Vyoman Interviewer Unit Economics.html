01 · Per-feature cost

Five features, five very different price tags

Every provider call anywhere in the product writes one usage_events row with its real cost in paise. These are the live averages read back from that table — not list prices, what actually got charged.

real Averages from the live project's usage_events table. Sample sizes vary a lot — interview cost rests on a 9-interview go/no-go test built for exactly this purpose; resume scan (n=6) and bullet rewrite (n=2) are real but thin samples from manual verification, not a volume test. Treat those two as directionally right, not statistically tight.

02 · Why the interview costs what it costs

Speech, not the LLM, is the bill

An interview calls three providers: Gemini for in-stage follow-ups, Groq for answer transcription, Sarvam for both answer-transcription fallback and every question's spoken audio. Sarvam alone is three-quarters of the per-interview cost.

03 · The platform bill

Infrastructure is flat, not per-student

Unlike AI calls, hosting is mostly a fixed monthly bill that doesn't move much between 100 students and 1,000 — each vendor's free tier or base plan absorbs this scale almost entirely.

estimated Compute Run, Resend and Upstash land inside their free tiers at 1,000 students on the usage model below — shown as ₹0, with the headroom noted. The one line that isn't flat: R2 answer-audio storage compounds every month since clips are kept for report playback and nothing expires them yet.
