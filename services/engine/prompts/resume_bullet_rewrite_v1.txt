did + measurable result" structure.

"""
{bullet}
"""

  pronouns ("I"/"we"), starting with a strong action verb.

Respond with strict JSON only, matching exactly one of these two shapes,
no other text:

{{"kind": "needs_metric", "question": "<the specific question to ask>"}}
